Parents' Top Questions — Our Honest Answers

Aapke genuine concerns ko ignore nahi karna chahiye. Isliye hum simple aur honest answers ke saath apni approach samjhate hain.

❓ Itni chhoti age mein AI ki kya zarurat hai?

AI expert banne ki zarurat nahi hai. Lekin curiosity, digital awareness aur critical thinking jitni early develop hogi, utna bachcha technology ko responsibly samajh paayega.

❓ AI se bachcha lazy nahi ho jayega?

Ho sakta hai agar AI ko homework shortcut ki tarah use kiya jaye. Isi liye hum AI + Critical Thinking approach rakhte hain — question, verification aur creation.

❓ Kya ye sirf ChatGPT sikhana hai?

Sirf ChatGPT nahi. AI kya hai, real life mein kahan use hota hai, information ko kaise question karein aur technology safely kaise use karein — ye broader learning hai.

❓ Mera bachcha computer properly nahi jaanta.

Koi problem nahi. Learning age-appropriate aur activity-based rakhi ja sakti hai. Basic computer understanding se gradually AI concepts ki taraf badha ja sakta hai.

❓ Iska benefit school mein kya hoga?

Observation, questioning, problem solving, creativity aur technology confidence jaise skills develop karne mein help mil sakti hai.

❓ AI ko blindly trust kyun nahi karna?

AI useful hai, lekin uska answer hamesha automatically correct nahi hota. Isliye bachchon ko verify karna, compare karna aur reason karna sikhana important hai.
