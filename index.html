FOR PARENTS ❤️

Questions Parents Ask. Answers We Believe In.

Aapke genuine concerns important hain. Hum hype ke bajay simple, honest aur practical approach ko prefer karte hain.

?Itni chhoti age mein AI ki kya zarurat hai?
AI expert banna zaroori nahi. Curiosity, digital awareness aur critical thinking early develop hone se bachcha technology ko more responsibly samajh sakta hai.
?AI se bachcha lazy nahi ho jayega?
Ho sakta hai agar AI ko homework shortcut ki tarah use kiya jaye. Isi liye hamara focus question, verification aur creation par hai.
?Kya ye sirf ChatGPT sikhana hai?
Nahi. AI kya hai, real life mein kahan use hota hai, information ko question kaise karein aur technology ko safely kaise use karein — ye broader learning hai.
?Mera bachcha computer properly nahi jaanta.
Koi problem nahi. Learning age-appropriate aur activity-based rakhi ja sakti hai. Basic computer understanding se gradually AI concepts ki taraf badha ja sakta hai.
?Iska benefit school mein kya hoga?
Observation, questioning, problem solving, creativity aur technology confidence jaise skills develop karne mein help mil sakti hai.
?AI ko blindly trust kyun nahi karna?
AI useful hai, lekin iska answer hamesha automatically correct nahi hota. Bachchon ko verify, compare aur reason karna sikhana important hai.
