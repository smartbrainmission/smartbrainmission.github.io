Parents' Top Questions — Our Honest Answers

Aapke genuine concerns ko ignore nahi karna chahiye. Isliye hum simple aur honest answers se approach samjhate hain.

❓ Itni chhoti age mein AI ki kya zarurat hai?

AI expert banne ki zarurat nahi hai. Lekin curiosity, digital awareness aur critical thinking jitni early develop hogi, utna bachcha technology ko responsibly samajh paayega.

❓ AI se bachcha lazy nahi ho jayega?

Ho sakta hai agar AI ko homework shortcut ki tarah use kiya jaye. Isi liye hum AI + Critical Thinking approach rakhte hain — question, verification aur creation.

❓ Kya ye sirf ChatGPT sikhana hai?

Sirf ChatGPT nahi. AI kya hai, real life mein kahan use hota hai, computer kaise think karta hai, information ko kaise question karein aur technology safely kaise use karein — ye broader learning hai.

❓ Mera bachcha abhi computer properly nahi jaanta.

Koi problem nahi. Learning ko age-appropriate aur activity-based rakha gaya hai. Basic computer understanding se gradually AI concepts ki taraf ja sakte hain.

❓ Iska benefit school mein kya hoga?

School subjects ke saath-saath observation, questioning, problem solving, creativity aur technology confidence jaise skills develop karne mein help mil sakti hai.

❓ AI ko homework machine banana hai?

Nahi. AI ko apne brain ka tool banana hai — answer lene se zyada important hai question karna, sochna, verify karna aur create karna.
